# Sermon reel hook and caption

Input: one approved reel script (sermon cut or Q&A), its source (speaker, date, title), and its filename stem.
Output: one markdown file `<stem>-hook-caption.md` with the sections below. Write in the script's language (usually Indonesian).

Do not post the captions or the video to social media. Hand the file to the owner for approval; posting is a separate, explicit request.

## 1. Find the essence first
Write one sentence: the single truth this script lands, and the question a viewer really asks themselves that it answers. Every hookline and the caption must serve this sentence. Never promise something the script does not deliver.

## 2. Hooklines (become the video cover)
The same video file goes to Instagram and TikTok, so the cover hook must fit the tighter limit.

| Use | Max words | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Cover text (Instagram Reels + TikTok, same cover) | 6 | Readable in under 2 seconds on a phone, clears TikTok's UI overlays |
| Threads first line (text above the video) | 12 | Threads shows text first, so a full short sentence works |

Write 3 of each kind for the cover, and 1 of each kind for Threads:
- **A. Straightforward**: says the point plainly, as the speaker means it.
- **B. Clickbait, never lying**: curiosity gap, surprising framing, a question, or a bold contrast, but the video must fully pay it off. No fake stakes, no words the speaker didn't mean, no misquoting Scripture.

Mark your recommended pick of each kind with a star and one line why.
Capitalize Allah, Tuhan, Yesus, Kristus, Roh Kudus, and Maha when it refers to God.

## 3. Captions
Gospel-centered: point to who God is and what Christ has done, not only to moral advice. Faithful to what the speaker actually said. Readable in 30 seconds or less. Fix punctuation and casing in the same way as the on-video captions.

- **Instagram**: max 90 words. Line 1 hook (can reuse a hookline), 2-4 short lines summarizing, one reflection question or invitation, credit line (speaker, church, service), then exactly 5 hashtags (Instagram's limit).
- **TikTok**: max 70 words, same structure but tighter, then 3-5 hashtags mixing one broad (#fyp-style or #renunganharian), one topic, one church tag.
- **Threads**: max 450 characters total (platform limit is 500), Threads first-line hook + 2-3 sentences + credit. One topic tag only (Threads allows one).

After writing, count words/characters and state them under each caption. Fix anything over.

## 4. Tags
Pick from: the sermon's theme, the Bible book or passage if one is central, the church/brand tag, and one or two discovery tags in the audience's language. No unrelated trending tags.

## 5. Hand off
Present the file for approval. The approved cover hookline goes to the cover and render steps as the cover text; the approved captions are ready if the owner later asks to post, and must be used unchanged at that point.
